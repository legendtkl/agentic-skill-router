# audio files: splitting audio on silence

## When to use

Use this skill for splitting audio on silence in audio files. This skill
specifically handles splitting audio on silence and nothing else in the
audio files family.

## Procedure

Detect silence and split a long recording into separate tracks at the gaps.

## Notes

This is the audio files skill dedicated to splitting audio on silence.
Sibling skills cover other audio files capabilities; this one
is the right choice only when the task is about splitting audio on silence.
