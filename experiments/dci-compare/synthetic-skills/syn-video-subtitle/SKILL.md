# video files: adding subtitles and captions

## When to use

Use this skill for adding subtitles and captions in video files. This skill
specifically handles adding subtitles and captions and nothing else in the
video files family.

## Procedure

Burn in or soft-mux subtitle tracks, sync timing, and convert between SRT/VTT.

## Notes

This is the video files skill dedicated to adding subtitles and captions.
Sibling skills cover other video files capabilities; this one
is the right choice only when the task is about adding subtitles and captions.
