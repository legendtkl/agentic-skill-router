# video files: trimming and cutting clips

## When to use

Use this skill for trimming and cutting clips in video files. This skill
specifically handles trimming and cutting clips and nothing else in the
video files family.

## Procedure

Cut a video to a start/end range, split into segments, and join clips without re-encoding when possible.

## Notes

This is the video files skill dedicated to trimming and cutting clips.
Sibling skills cover other video files capabilities; this one
is the right choice only when the task is about trimming and cutting clips.
