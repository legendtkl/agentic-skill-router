# video files: extracting thumbnails and frames

## When to use

Use this skill for extracting thumbnails and frames in video files. This skill
specifically handles extracting thumbnails and frames and nothing else in the
video files family.

## Procedure

Grab a frame at a timestamp, generate a contact sheet, and pick representative thumbnails.

## Notes

This is the video files skill dedicated to extracting thumbnails and frames.
Sibling skills cover other video files capabilities; this one
is the right choice only when the task is about extracting thumbnails and frames.
