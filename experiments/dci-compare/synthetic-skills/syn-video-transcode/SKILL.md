# video files: transcoding codecs and resolutions

## When to use

Use this skill for transcoding codecs and resolutions in video files. This skill
specifically handles transcoding codecs and resolutions and nothing else in the
video files family.

## Procedure

Transcode between codecs/containers, change resolution and bitrate, and target device-friendly profiles.

## Notes

This is the video files skill dedicated to transcoding codecs and resolutions.
Sibling skills cover other video files capabilities; this one
is the right choice only when the task is about transcoding codecs and resolutions.
